## 摘要
本文提出了一种堆叠式双层力敏电阻（FSR）阵列，设计用于机器人指尖，将高分辨率压力映射与剪切力估计相结合。柔顺的晶格弹性体间隔层将剪切载荷转化为可测量的层间位移，产生层间相对压力中心（CoP）偏移。基于物理的力矩平衡将层间CoP位移与剪切力联系起来，而端到端的CNN-GRU模型捕捉来自载荷相关压缩和接触再分布的非线性效应。该模型以两层为输入，对$F_x$的决定系数$R^2 = 0.914$，对$F_y$的$R^2 = 0.944$，在剪切力估计上始终优于单层基线方法。机器人操作实验表明，在接触运动跟踪中，深层跟踪机械臂施加的平移和旋转，而表层跟踪接触表面的滑移。两层总压力响应之差的瞬态变化在所测试的线索中提供了最佳的滑移事件检测性能。这些结果表明，双层FSR阵列能够提供三轴力估计、接触运动跟踪和滑移事件检测，超越了传统的法向力传感。

## Abstract
This paper presents a stacked two-layer force-sensing resistor (FSR) array designed for robotic fingertips that combines high-resolution pressure mapping with shear-force estimation. A compliant lattice elastomer spacer converts shear loading into a measurable inter-layer displacement, producing relative center-of-pressure (CoP) shifts between layers. A physics-based moment balance links inter-layer CoP displacement to shear force, while an end-to-end CNN--GRU model captures nonlinear effects from load-dependent compression and contact redistribution. This model, with both layers as input, achieves coefficients of determination $R^2 = 0.914$ for $F_x$ and $R^2 = 0.944$ for $F_y$, consistently outperforming single-layer baselines for shear-force estimation. Robotic manipulation experiments show that, for contact-motion tracking, the deep layer tracks the translation and rotation imposed by the robot arm, whereas the superficial layer tracks the slip at the contact surface. Transient changes in the difference between the total pressure responses of the two layers provide the best slip-event detection performance among the tested cues. These results demonstrate that two-layer FSR arrays can provide three-axis force estimation, contact-motion tracking, and slip-event detection beyond conventional normal-force sensing.
