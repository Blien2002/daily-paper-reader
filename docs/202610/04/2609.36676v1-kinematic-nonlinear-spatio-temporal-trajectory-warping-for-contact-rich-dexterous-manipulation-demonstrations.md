## 摘要
我们提出了一种简单而有效的方法，用于重新利用现有的接触丰富灵巧操作演示。从手和物体轨迹的输入开始，我们的方法输出高质量的非线性轨迹扭曲，这些扭曲考虑了中间路径点、环境障碍、时间偏移以及变化的起始/结束配置。我们方法的基础是利用接触分布，我们表明这使得我们能够按照简单的以物体为中心的扭曲规范流程，可靠地计算复杂且高维的灵巧手轨迹。我们在公开的人手运动数据集中来自4个演示的12个变体上评估了我们的方法，进行了基线比较，并证明了我们的方法对不同操作器的泛化能力。结果和代码将在发表时提供。

## Abstract
We present a straightforward but effective method for repurposing existing contact-rich dexterous manipulation demonstrations. Starting from inputs of hand and object trajectories, our method outputs high-quality nonlinear trajectory warps that account for intermediate waypoints, environmental barriers, temporal shifts, and varied start/end configurations. Foundational to our method is the utilization of contact distributions, which we show allows us to reliably compute complex and high-dimensional dexterous hand trajectories following a simple object-centric warp specification pipeline. We evaluate our method across 12 variations sourced from 4 demonstrations in a publicly available dataset of human hand motion data, perform baseline comparisons, and demonstrate generalization of our approach to different manipulators. Results and code will be made available on publication.
