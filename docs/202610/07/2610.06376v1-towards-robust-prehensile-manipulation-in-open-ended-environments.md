## 摘要
我们提出使用质量-多样性（Quality-Diversity，QD）算法来解决开放式环境中的机器人抓取操作任务。我们的方法能够高效地发现多种稳健的抓取构型，这些构型可作为在关节化物体上生成多样化抓取操作轨迹的可靠起点。由此产生的操作行为多样性增强了泛化能力和适应性，使其能够有效部署于持续演化的开放世界环境中。

## Abstract
We propose to use Quality-Diversity (QD) algorithms to solve robotic prehensile manipulation tasks in open-ended environments. Our approach enables the efficient discovery of a wide range robust grasp configurations, which serve as reliable starting points for generating diverse prehensile manipulation trajectories on articulated objects. The resulting diversity in manipulation behaviors enhances generalization and adaptability, enabling effective deployment continuously evolving open-world settings.
