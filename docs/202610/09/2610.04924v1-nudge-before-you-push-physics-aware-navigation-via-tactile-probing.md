## 摘要
视觉上相同的容器可能隐藏着需要不同搬运决策的载荷。我们提出了 TANav，该方法利用一次短暂轻推测量推动阻力，在场地定义的搬运边界下进行导航。TacPhys 读取力序列，并可结合 RGB-D 和运动学信息，得到用于推动授权的质量估计。重复巡逻规划器权衡探测代价和路径代价，在有用时请求第二次接触，并在多次巡视间重用观测。在模拟中，TacPhys 接近仅基于阻力的贝叶斯参考方法，在可比的低风险工作点下，相对于峰值力阈值法将漏推率从 28.7% 降至 5.5%。在重复巡逻模拟中，TANav 恢复了 oracle 路径节省的 90%，相对于始终绕行将人工干预减少一半以上，相对于仅 RGB-D 探测将边界违反率从 4.3% 降至 2.9%。在配备霍尔阵列指尖的四足机械臂上，对于重量不超过 2.82 kg 的容器，离线零样本 MAE 为 0.28–1.07 kg。在 3 kg 负载下进行力上升校准，汇总离线准确率为 93.5%（非立方体场景为 86.4%）；单独的原始峰值规则在未见过的箱体上给出 20 次在线决策中的 15 次正确结果。

## Abstract
Visually identical containers can conceal loads that require different handling decisions. We present TANav, which uses a brief nudge to measure push resistance for navigation under a site-defined handling boundary. TacPhys reads the force sequence, with optional RGB-D and kinematics, into a mass estimate for push authorization. A repeated-patrol planner weighs probe and route costs, requests a second contact when useful, and reuses observations across visits. In simulation, TacPhys approaches a resistance-only Bayes reference and reduces missed pushes from 28.7% to 5.5% relative to peak-force thresholding at comparable low-risk operating points. In repeated-patrol simulation, TANav recovers 90% of the oracle's path saving, more than halves human interventions relative to always-detour, and reduces boundary violations from 4.3% to 2.9% relative to RGB-D-only probing. On a quadruped manipulator with a Hall-array fingertip, offline zero-shot MAE is 0.28-1.07 kg on containers up to 2.82 kg. Force-rise calibration at 3 kg gives 93.5% pooled offline accuracy (86.4% on non-cube episodes); a separate raw-peak rule gives 15 of 20 correct online decisions on unseen boxes.
