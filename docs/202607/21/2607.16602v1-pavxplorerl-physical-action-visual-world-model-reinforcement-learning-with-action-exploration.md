## 摘要
动作条件世界模型是具身智能的关键组成部分，作为可扩展的策略评估器，可减少对昂贵的真实世界部署的依赖。为了准确捕捉由不同动作引发的动态变化，此类模型应满足三个关键目标——物理合理性（P）、动作遵循性（A）和视觉逼真度（V），统称为PAV——同时要既能鲁棒处理分布内（ID）专家演示，也能处理分布外（OOD）动作。然而，现有方法主要依赖ID动作-视频对和像素级重建损失，这并未显式优化PAV目标，且对专家数据以外的泛化能力较差。为解决此问题，我们提出PAVXploreRL，一种基于预训练潜在世界模型的强化学习框架，通过奖励驱动训练显式优化PAV目标。为提升动作泛化能力，我们的方法联合利用ID轨迹和噪声驱动的OOD动作探索，无需成对的视频监督。实验表明，PAVXploreRL持续优于预训练基线，在多个基准测试上平均提升5.6%，并产生更高质量的PAV属性。作为策略评估器，它也提供更可靠的性能估计，并减少如Ctrl-World等先前仅使用专家数据的世界模型的过估计偏差。代码：https://github.com/Social-AI-Studio/PAVXploreRL

## Abstract
Action-conditioned world models are a key component of embodied AI, serving as scalable policy evaluators that reduce reliance on expensive real-world rollouts. To accurately capture diverse action-induced dynamics, such models should satisfy three key objectives-Physical Plausibility (P), Action Adherence (A), and Visual Fidelity (V), collectively referred to as PAV-while remaining robust to both in-distribution (ID) expert demonstrations and out-of-distribution (OOD) actions. However, existing methods primarily rely on ID action-video pairs and pixel-level reconstruction losses, which do not explicitly optimize PAV objectives and generalize poorly beyond expert data. To address this, we propose PAVXploreRL, a reinforcement learning framework built on a pretrained latent world model that explicitly optimizes PAV objectives through reward-driven training. To improve action generalization, our method jointly leverages ID trajectories and noise-driven OOD action exploration, without paired video supervision. Experiments show that PAVXploreRL consistently outperforms pretrained baselines, achieving a 5.6% average gain across benchmarks and producing higher-quality PAV properties. As a policy evaluator, it also yields more reliable performance estimates and reduces the overestimation bias of prior expert-only world models such as Ctrl-World.
